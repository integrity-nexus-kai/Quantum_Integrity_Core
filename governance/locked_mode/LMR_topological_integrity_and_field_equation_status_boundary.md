## 2. Working semantic clarification: “Topological”

Within the current TIG research context, **topological** refers to structural relations such as:

- connectivity,
- adjacency,
- boundaries,
- orientation,
- continuity classes,
- and admissible mappings or transitions,

considered prior to, or independently from, a fully specified metric description based on distances, angles, and curvature.

This working clarification does **not** by itself establish a formal topological model for TIG.

It identifies the intended research direction:

> determine whether admissible structural relations can constrain or generate the later emergence of geometry, spacetime, and gravitational behavior.

---

## 3. Working semantic clarification: “Integrity”

Within TIG, **integrity** is not a moral or ethical term.

It refers to structural self-consistency and admissibility, including the preservation or controlled transformation of constitutive relations, constraints, and allowed state transitions.

A working combined formulation is:

> **Topological integrity** denotes the preservation, compatibility, or admissible transformation of fundamental structural relations under allowed state transitions.

This formulation remains a governance-level working definition until a canonical mathematical glossary is established.

These points are registered only as **exploratory research hypotheses**.
