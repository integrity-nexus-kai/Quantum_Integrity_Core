# Integrity Tensor Candidates

## Purpose

This document serves as the official registry of all Integrity Tensor realizations proposed within the TIG framework.

Its purpose is not to establish a canonical tensor.

Its purpose is to track, classify, and evaluate candidate realizations.

Only candidates that satisfy the Integrity Tensor Requirements may eventually be considered for canonical adoption.

---

## Classification Rules

Every candidate must be assigned one of the following states:

### Exploratory

Initial idea.

No derivation.

No validation.

---

### Candidate

Explicit mathematical form exists.

Partial analysis completed.

Validation incomplete.

---

### Advanced Candidate

Mathematical structure established.

Major consistency checks completed.

Validation ongoing.

---

### Validated

All tensor requirements satisfied.

Independent verification completed.

Eligible for canonical review.

---

### Canonical

Accepted as part of the TIG framework.
