## Purpose

ITC-004 investigates whether the Integrity Tensor may emerge as an effective stress structure associated with integrity-preserving geometric organization.

Unlike previous candidates, this approach does not begin from admissibility, connectivity, or volumetric organization directly.

Instead, it examines whether integrity manifests as an effective source term within the gravitational field equations.

No explicit tensor realization currently exists.

This document serves as a research registration.

---

## Motivation

The current TIG realization can be interpreted as producing an effective geometric response.

This raises the possibility that:

```text
Integrity
        ↓
Effective Stress Structure
        ↓
Geometry
        ↓
Curvature
        ↓
Dynamics
```

rather than:

```text

        ↓
Geometry
```

The central question is whether integrity behaves mathematically as an effective stress source.
