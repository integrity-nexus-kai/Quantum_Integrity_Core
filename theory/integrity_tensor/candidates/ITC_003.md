## Purpose

ITC-003 investigates whether the Integrity Tensor can emerge from volumetric organization.

This candidate explores the hypothesis that physical geometry is not fundamentally generated by curvature alone, but by the organization of volumetric structure.

No explicit tensor realization currently exists.

This document serves as a research registration.

---

## Motivation

Within the TIG development chain, volumetric organization occupies a central intermediate position between connectivity and geometry.

The conceptual sequence is:

```text
Structural Admissibility
        ↓
Connectivity Organization
        ↓
Volumetric Organization
        ↓
Metric Emergence
        ↓
Curvature Structure
        ↓
Gravitational Dynamics
```

The unresolved question is whether volumetric organization can serve as the mathematical source of the Integrity Tensor.

---

## Core Hypothesis

Physical geometry may emerge from the organization of admissible volume rather than from curvature alone.

If true:

```text
Volumetric Organization
        ↓
Tensor Structure
        ↓
Metric Structure
        ↓
Curvature
        ↓
Dynamics
```

The Integrity Tensor would therefore encode volumetric organization.
