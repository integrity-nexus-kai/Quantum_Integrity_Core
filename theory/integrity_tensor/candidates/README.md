# Integrity Tensor Candidate Registry

## Purpose

This registry maintains all candidate realizations of the TIG Integrity Tensor.

Its purpose is not to establish a canonical tensor.

Its purpose is to provide a controlled evaluation framework through which tensor candidates can be proposed, analyzed, validated, rejected, or promoted.

---

## Registry Principle

The Integrity Tensor remains an unresolved mathematical object.

Therefore:

```text
No canonical tensor currently exists.
```

All tensor realizations must first enter the candidate registry.

Only after satisfying all tensor requirements may a candidate be considered for canonical review.

---

## Candidate Classification

### Exploratory

Initial mathematical idea.

No derivation.

No validation.

---

### Candidate

Explicit tensor structure proposed.

Partial analysis completed.

Validation incomplete.

---

### Advanced Candidate

Major consistency tests completed.

Requirements substantially satisfied.

Independent review pending.

---

### Validated

All requirements satisfied.

Mathematical consistency established.

Eligible for canonical review.

---

### Canonical

Officially adopted by the TIG framework.

## Evaluation Procedure

Every candidate must be evaluated against:

- General Relativity Recovery
- Newtonian Recovery
- Covariance
- Conservation Consistency
- Finite Curvature Compatibility
- Horizon Emergence
- Vacuum Compatibility
- Structural Admissibility

Failure of any requirement may result in rejection.
