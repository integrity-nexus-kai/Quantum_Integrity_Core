## Purpose

ITC-002 investigates whether the Integrity Tensor can emerge directly from the TIG concept of structural admissibility.

Unlike ITC-001, which begins with connectivity organization, this candidate treats admissibility itself as the fundamental mathematical object.

No explicit tensor realization currently exists.

This document serves as a research registration.

---

## Motivation

The oldest and most stable concept within TIG is:

```text
Admissibility
```

The framework originally emerged from the hypothesis that not every mathematically possible geometry should be considered physically admissible.

The central question therefore becomes:

```text
Can admissibility itself be represented as a tensor structure?
```

---

## Core Hypothesis

Admissibility may be a fundamental geometric property.

If true:

```text
Admissibility
        ↓
Tensor Structure
        ↓
Geometry
        ↓
Curvature
        ↓
Dynamics
```

rather than:

```text
Geometry
        ↓
Admissibility
```
