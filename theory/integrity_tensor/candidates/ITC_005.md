## Purpose

ITC-005 investigates whether the Integrity Tensor can emerge from a variational principle.

Unlike the previous candidates, this approach begins with the assumption that integrity is encoded within an action functional.

The Integrity Tensor would then arise through variation of the action.

No action principle currently exists.

No tensor realization currently exists.

This document serves as a research registration.

---

## Motivation

One of the major unresolved questions of the TIG framework is:

```text
Does TIG possess a variational formulation?
```

Most successful physical theories derive their field equations from an action principle.

This motivates the investigation of whether integrity can be represented within an admissibility-driven action.

---

## Core Hypothesis

The Integrity Tensor may emerge through variation of an integrity-based action.

Conceptually:

```text
Integrity Action
        ↓
Variation
        ↓
Integrity Tensor
        ↓
Field Equation
        ↓
Geometry
```

rather than:

```text
Tensor
        ↓
Field Equation
```

The tensor would therefore not be fundamental.

It would be derived.
