# Integrity Tensor Program

## Purpose

The Integrity Tensor Program defines the current research objective of the TIG framework concerning the derivation of a mathematically admissible Integrity Tensor.

The purpose of this program is not to propose a final tensor realization.

The purpose is to establish the research route leading from TIG foundations toward a covariant tensor formulation.

---

## Current Situation

TIG presently possesses:

- a validated horizon structure,
- a bounded-curvature representative geometry,
- a structural admissibility framework,
- a field-equation architecture,
- and a set of tensor requirements.

However, the Integrity Tensor itself has not yet been derived.

The tensor remains the central unresolved mathematical object of the TIG Field Equation Program.

---

## Program Objective

Derive a tensor

```text
Iμν
```

such that:

```text
Gμν = Iμν
```

represents a mathematically consistent extension of the current TIG framework.

The tensor must emerge from TIG foundations rather than being introduced phenomenologically.
