# Core Research Question

Can geometry emerge from the organization of dimensional relationships?

---

# Motivation

Dimension alone does not define geometry.

Two systems may both possess three dimensions while exhibiting fundamentally different geometric behavior.

Examples include:

- Euclidean geometry,
- spherical geometry,
- hyperbolic geometry.

Dimension specifies the number of organizational freedoms.

Geometry specifies how those freedoms are arranged.

---

# The Geometry Problem

Dimension determines:

- available directions,
- scaling freedom,
- organizational expansion.

However, dimension alone does not determine:

- shape,
- curvature,
- neighborhood organization,
- global structure.

Additional organization is required.
