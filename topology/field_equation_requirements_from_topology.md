Can the requirements of a gravitational field equation be reconstructed from topological foundations?

## Requirement 1: Structural Consistency

A valid field equation must preserve organizational admissibility.

Physical evolution must not generate structurally inadmissible states.

The equation must therefore respect the admissibility structure of the underlying system.

---

## Requirement 2: Persistence Preservation

A valid field equation must preserve persistent organization.

Evolution must not destroy the conditions necessary for stable physical structure.

Persistent organization remains a foundational constraint.

---

## Requirement 3: Connectivity Compatibility

Connectivity acts as the precursor to geometry.

A valid field equation must therefore remain compatible with admissible connectivity structure.

Geometric evolution cannot violate the organizational relationships from which geometry emerges.

---

## Requirement 4: Metric Compatibility

The field equation must preserve the consistency of emergent metric structure.

Distance relationships must remain physically meaningful throughout evolution.

Metric breakdown would imply organizational breakdown.

---

## Requirement 5: Curvature Emergence

Curvature should arise from connectivity variation.

A valid field equation must therefore permit curvature to emerge as a consequence of organizational structure rather than treating curvature as completely independent.

---

## Requirement 6: Critical Transition Compatibility

The TIG framework contains critical transitions.

A valid field equation must permit:

- stability regions,
- transition regions,
- critical thresholds.

Critical behavior must emerge naturally from the governing dynamics.

---

## Requirement 7: Horizon Compatibility

The TIG framework contains admissible horizon structures.

A valid field equation must permit:

- horizon formation,
- horizon transitions,
- admissible horizon sectors.

The field equation must therefore remain compatible with the TIG horizon program.

---

## Requirement 8: General Relativity Recovery

Any admissible TIG field equation must recover General Relativity within appropriate limits.

In regimes where organizational corrections become negligible:

TIG

↓

General Relativity

The low-curvature regime must remain observationally consistent.

---

## Requirement 9: Singular Structure Resolution

Traditional gravitational descriptions permit singular structures.

The TIG program investigates an alternative possibility:

Critical Transition

↓

Admissible Reorganization

rather than:

Collapse

↓

Singularity

A future field equation should remain compatible with this research direction.

---

## Requirement 10: Observational Consistency

A valid field equation must ultimately produce observable consequences.

Theoretical consistency alone is insufficient.

Physical observability remains mandatory.
