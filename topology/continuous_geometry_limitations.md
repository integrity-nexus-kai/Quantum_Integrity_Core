# Continuous Geometry Limitations

## Status

Open Foundational Investigation

Part of the TIG Topological Foundations Program.

---

# Core Question

Can a purely continuous geometric description fully account for persistence, integrity, and stable state organization?

---

# Motivation

General Relativity successfully describes gravitational phenomena through smooth differentiable geometry.

However, several persistent conceptual challenges appear in continuous descriptions:

- singularities,
- divergent quantities,
- information preservation questions,
- horizon pathologies,
- breakdown of physical interpretation in extreme regimes.

The purpose of this investigation is not to reject continuous geometry.

The purpose is to determine whether continuity alone is sufficient.

---

# Working Hypothesis H1

A purely continuous geometric description may be insufficient to explain persistent physical organization.

---

# Working Hypothesis H2

Persistence and structural integrity may impose additional organizational requirements beyond smooth geometry.

---

# Singularity Problem

Continuous compression can generate formally divergent states.

This raises a structural question:

> Does admissible reality require mechanisms that prevent unrestricted collapse?

If so, continuity alone may not provide the full organizational description.

---

# Persistence Problem

A physically admissible universe must preserve stable state relationships.

The existence of persistent structure may require organizational constraints not explicitly contained within continuous geometry.

---

# Addressability Problem

Persistent state organization requires distinguishable state relationships.

This motivates investigation of whether purely continuous descriptions are sufficient for stable state identification.

---

# Structural Integrity Problem

TIG proposes that reality is constrained by structural admissibility.

The question therefore becomes:

> Can structural integrity emerge solely from continuity, or does it require additional geometric organization?

---

# Expected Outcome

This investigation seeks to determine whether admissibility requirements imply the necessity of volumetric organizational structures.

---

# Connection to Future Work

If continuity alone proves insufficient, the next research stage becomes:

Volumetric Organization Necessity.

This forms the bridge toward investigation of emergent three-dimensional structural organization.
