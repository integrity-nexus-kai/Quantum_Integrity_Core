## Status

Canonical Topological Foundations

This document defines the current canonical emergence route of the Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG) research program.

The route summarizes how observable gravitational structure may emerge from deeper organizational principles.

It serves as the central dependency chain connecting all major TIG foundations investigations.

## Core Principle

TIG investigates the possibility that gravitational structure is not fundamental.

Instead, geometry may emerge from deeper layers of organizational admissibility.

The central question therefore becomes:

> What is the minimal structure required for stable, persistent, observable organization?

## Stage 1: Structural Admissibility

A structure may exist only if its internal relationships remain mutually admissible.

Non-admissible configurations cannot persist.

Structural admissibility therefore acts as the primary selection principle.

---

## Stage 2: Persistent Organization

Admissible structures may generate persistent organization.

Organization is defined as the preservation of distinguishable relationships through time.

Without persistence, no stable structure exists.

---

## Stage 3: Volumetric Organization

Persistent recursive organization generates increasing relational complexity.

Surface-based organization eventually encounters organizational saturation.

Volumetric organization provides additional organizational capacity.

TIG therefore investigates volume as a structural necessity rather than a geometric assumption.

---

## Stage 4: Connectivity

Volume alone does not generate structure.

Organization requires relationships between organizational units.

Connectivity determines:

- which states may interact,
- which transitions are admissible,
- which structures remain persistent.

Connectivity therefore becomes the organizational substrate of higher structure.

---

## Stage 5: Metric Structure

Connectivity relations may generate effective notions of accessibility and separation.

Metric structure is interpreted as a large-scale description of connectivity relationships.

Under this interpretation, distance is not fundamental.

Distance emerges.

---

## Stage 6: Geometry

Once metric relations exist, geometric structures become definable.

Neighborhoods, paths, separation, and accessibility become observable geometric concepts.

Geometry therefore emerges from metric organization.

---

## Stage 7: Curvature

Variations in geometric structure generate curvature.

Curvature becomes a large-scale manifestation of underlying organizational structure.

Under the TIG research program, curvature is therefore interpreted as an emergent quantity.

---

## Stage 8: Gravitational Dynamics

Curvature governs gravitational dynamics.

Observable gravitational phenomena arise at this level.

The TIG horizon structure and critical transition framework are interpreted as manifestations of this emergent hierarchy.

---

## Relation to General Relativity

General Relativity begins with geometry and curvature.

TIG investigates whether geometry itself may emerge from deeper organizational principles.

The two approaches therefore operate at different explanatory levels.

General Relativity describes gravitational dynamics.

TIG investigates the origin of the structures from which those dynamics arise.
