# TIG Topological Foundations Program

## Purpose

The Topological Foundations Program extends the structural admissibility framework of TIG beyond horizon formation.

The central research question is:

> Which minimal geometric structures are capable of supporting persistent admissible states?

The program investigates whether structural admissibility alone imposes constraints on the organization of physical space-time.

---

6. Minimal Structure Comparison
7. Cube Necessity Investigation
8. Emergent 3D Space-Time Structure
9. TIG Topological Extension

---

## Current Status

Open foundational research program.

No specific geometric structure is assumed a priori.

All candidate structures must emerge from admissibility, persistence, integrity, recursion, and state-organization requirements.

---

## Strategic Goal

To determine whether the observed organization of space-time can be derived from structural admissibility principles rather than postulated geometrically.
