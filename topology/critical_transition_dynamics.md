A fundamental question remains:

# Core Research Question

Can horizon formation be understood as a critical organizational transition?

---

# Motivation

Many physical systems exhibit critical behavior:

- phase transitions,
- bifurcations,
- stability loss,
- topology changes.

The Topological Foundations Program explores whether horizon formation belongs to this broader class of critical phenomena.

---

# The Transition Problem

A system may evolve continuously while its observable behavior changes discontinuously.

Examples include:

- liquid to gas,
- magnetic transitions,
- stability bifurcations.

This suggests that horizon formation may not require a continuous geometric mechanism.

Instead, horizon formation may emerge through critical organizational dynamics.

---

# Working Hypothesis H3

# Critical Threshold Principle

There exists a critical organizational condition:

C = Ccritical

Below the threshold:

- organization remains globally accessible.

Above the threshold:

- organization enters a qualitatively different regime.

This transition becomes a candidate mechanism for horizon formation.
