# Cube Necessity Investigation

## Status

Open Foundational Investigation

Part of the TIG Topological Foundations Program.

---

# Purpose

The Minimal Structure Comparison investigation identifies the cube as a leading candidate for persistent admissible organization.

The purpose of the present investigation is to determine whether this preference is contingent or necessary.

The central question becomes:

> Is the cube merely a useful structure, or does it emerge as the unique solution to the requirements of admissible persistent organization?

---

# Motivation

Previous investigations established:

Structural Admissibility
→ Persistence Requirements
→ Volumetric Organization Necessity
→ Minimal Structure Comparison

The current investigation focuses on determining whether cubic organization possesses unique properties not simultaneously achievable by competing structures.

---

# Core Research Question

Can the cube be derived from admissibility requirements?

Or is it merely one candidate among many?

---

# Necessary Properties

Any successful candidate must simultaneously satisfy:

- Persistence
- Integrity
- Connectivity
- Addressability
- Recursive Growth
- Organizational Simplicity

Failure in any category disqualifies the candidate.

---

# Cube Property P1

## Exact Volumetric Tiling

The cube fills three-dimensional space without gaps.

No additional transition structures are required.

This minimizes organizational overhead.

---

# Cube Property P2

## Stable Neighborhood Structure

Each cube possesses a fixed local neighborhood relation.

This supports:

- state propagation,
- local interaction,
- recursive extension,
- structural coherence.

---

# Cube Property P3

## Direct Addressability

Cubic organization naturally supports coordinate assignment.

Every state can be uniquely referenced.

This significantly reduces organizational complexity.

---

# Cube Property P4

## Recursive Extension

Cubic structures can be extended indefinitely without changing local rules.

The same organizational principles apply at all scales.

---

# Cube Property P5

## Organizational Economy

The cube appears to minimize:

- coordination requirements,
- structural bookkeeping,
- transition complexity,
- neighborhood ambiguity.

This property may be fundamental.

---

# The Organizational Cost Principle

Working Hypothesis:

> The preferred persistent structure minimizes the cost of maintaining admissible organization.

This principle is independent of geometry itself.

Geometry becomes a consequence of organizational efficiency.
